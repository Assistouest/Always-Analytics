cloud-provider-ip-addresses-derived range data
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Project:   cloud-provider-ip-addresses
Source:    https://github.com/rezmoss/cloud-provider-ip-addresses
Location:  data/datacenter-v4-ranges.txt
           data/datacenter-v6-ranges.txt
License:   CC0-1.0
Generated: 2026-07-18

The bundled files contain normalized inclusive hexadecimal ranges derived from
public cloud-provider address lists aggregated by the upstream project. The
following providers were included in the source dataset: AWS, Azure, Google
Cloud, OVHcloud, Hetzner, Scaleway, DigitalOcean, Linode, Vultr, Oracle,
Alibaba, Leaseweb, UpCloud, Tencent, Huawei, IBM Cloud, Gcore, and Rackspace.
Consumer VPN providers, iCloud Private Relay, and Tor were intentionally not
included.
